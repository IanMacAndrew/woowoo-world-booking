3. Why we collect it

  • To process and confirm your booking, and administer the training session (registration lists, name badges, certificates).
  • To process payment securely through Stripe.
  • To send booking confirmations, invoices, and pre-event information by email.
  • To provide documentation your organisation may need for an HRD Corp claim.
  • To keep the accounting and tax records we're legally required to keep.
  • To track which sales representative referred a booking, for internal commission purposes only.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for advertising or marketing to third parties.

5. How long we keep it

We retain booking and payment records for as long as required for accounting and tax purposes under Malaysian law (typically 7 years from the relevant transaction). Delegate contact details used only for pre-event communication are deleted or anonymised once no longer needed for that purpose, unless a longer retention period is required for the reasons above.

6. Your rights under the PDPA

Under the Personal Data Protection Act 2010, you have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate personal data.
  • Withdraw consent to our processing of your data, where consent is the basis for that processing (note: this may affect our ability to complete an active booking).
  • Request that we limit further processing of your data in certain circumstances.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in Section 9.

7. Security

We use industry-standard technical measures — including encrypted connections (HTTPS) and access-controlled cloud storage — to protect the personal data we hold. Payment details are handled entirely by Stripe, a PCI-DSS compliant payment processor, and never touch our own servers.

8. Cookies

Our website does not currently use advertising or tracking cookies. Our hosting and security providers (Cloudflare, Netlify) may set minimal technical cookies necessary for the site to function securely — these do not identify you personally.
