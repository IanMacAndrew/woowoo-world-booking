AI sorts two ways: by capability (how powerful it is) and by functionality (how it works). The primary split by capability is Narrow AI — built for specific tasks — versus Artificial General Intelligence, AGI. Our 1-Day and 2-Day formats focus on Narrow AI: practical, task-level wins you can apply immediately. The Masterclass goes further — re-engineering how your organisation's systems work, to actually adopt AGI. That's why Strategy leads everything we teach: Sales & Marketing, HR Development and Resilience are important, narrow-focused add-ons underneath it, not the main event.
