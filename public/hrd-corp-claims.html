How HRD Corp's Claims Work

A plain-language summary for prospective bookers — see hrdcorp.gov.my for the definitive rules that apply to your organisation.

HRD Corp (the Human Resource Development Corporation of Malaysia) lets levy-registered employers claim back eligible training costs from the levy they already pay. Our Strategic AI Sales & Marketing programmes — Briefing, Deep Dive, Masterclass and Bootcamp — are structured as claimable training, so your organisation may be able to recover some or all of what you spend booking a seat.

What HRD Corp can cover

For in-house training conducted at an external venue, HRD Corp's claim typically covers two things, each capped by daily allowances:

Cost typeTypical cap
Hotel Rental Package / Rental of Training Place Subject to a daily allowance or group venue limit — roughly RM150 to RM400 per person, per day, depending on travel distance to the venue
Participant daily allowance RM250/day if travelling under 100km to the venue, up to RM500/day if travelling over 100km — intended to cover combined hotel stays and meals
These figures are indicative caps, not fixed reimbursement amounts — actual claimable amounts depend on your organisation's HRD Corp levy balance, the specific claim category used, and HRD Corp's rules at the time of claim. We'll help with the documentation, but the claim itself is between your organisation and HRD Corp.

The general process

  1. Your organisation books seats on a WooWoo World Strategic AI Sales & Marketing programme.
  2. We provide the documentation your HR/training team needs to support a claim (invoice, programme outline, attendance confirmation).
  3. Your organisation submits the claim to HRD Corp through their e-TRiS system, within HRD Corp's stipulated claim window.
  4. HRD Corp assesses the claim against your levy balance and the applicable caps, and reimburses your organisation directly.

Where to go for definitive answers

Eligibility, exact reimbursement amounts, and claim deadlines depend on your organisation's specific circumstances. Your HR or training department, or HRD Corp directly, are the authoritative sources — we can only speak to how our programmes are structured to support a claim.
